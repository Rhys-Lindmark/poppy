# Personal Agent Protocol

Poppy defines how software acting for a person discovers and interacts with a company.

## Parties

**User**:
The person on whose behalf the Personal Agent acts.

**Personal Agent**:
Software acting on behalf of a User.

**Company**:
An organization that offers one or more ways for a Personal Agent to interact with it through Poppy.

**Company Agent**:
The Company's representative in a Poppy conversation.

## Discovery and access

**Discovery Document (`poppy.json`)**:
A public declaration of the Company's identity and the ways a Personal Agent can interact with it.

**Session**:
One User's activity with one Company through a Personal Agent, across the channels the Company offers.

**Direct Sign-In**:
The User signs in with the Company and grants the Personal Agent access to their account.

## Cookbooks

**Cookbook**:
A worked example of a Poppy task or integration with an outcome an agent can verify.

## PoppyVal

**Conformance Check**:
A test of one Poppy requirement for a specified protocol version and participant role.

**Sales Coverage**:
The online retail sales represented by storefronts that meet a stated Poppy check, expressed in dollars and as a share of a defined market.
